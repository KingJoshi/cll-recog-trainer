Privacy Policy

Speedcube Trainer (cubing.cc) · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Plain-language summary: Speedcube Trainer is a free, non-commercial speedcubing training web app. We do not sell or rent your data, show ads, use third-party analytics or tracking cookies, or operate a backend server that collects or stores your personal information.

1. Who We Are & No Data Selling or Tracking

Speedcube Trainer, accessible at https://cubing.cc, is a free client-side training tool for practicing speedcubing recognition, algorithms, solves, and smart cube replays.

  • We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade any user data.
  • We do not run advertisements, behavioral profiling, third-party analytics scripts, or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We do not operate a developer backend database containing your solve times or Google account data.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

By default, all training data remains strictly inside your own web browser using standard browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, and first-party preference cookies strictly necessary for local persistence):

  • Solve times, penalties (+2, DNF), timestamps, scrambles, and optional solve notes/tags.
  • Training sessions, custom case labels, preferred algorithms, and recognition drill statistics.
  • Display preferences, active puzzle/track selection, and theme settings.

This local data never leaves your device unless you manually export a backup file or explicitly enable optional Google Drive Cloud Sync.

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Google Drive Sync

You can use Speedcube Trainer without signing in. If you choose to click Sign in with Google to synchronize settings and solve history across your devices or browser tabs, Speedcube Trainer uses Google Identity Services in your browser with only these minimal scopes:

  • openid, email, profile: Used solely to display your Google account name, email address, and profile avatar inside the Cloud Sync dialog so you know which account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: Grants access strictly to the hidden, application-specific App Data folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive to read and write a single sync file named speedcube-trainer-sync.json. Speedcube Trainer cannot view, list, read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

Communication happens directly between your browser and Google's APIs over HTTPS. Your OAuth access token is kept only in your browser session storage (with temporary in-browser fallback state for syncing open tabs on the same origin) and is never sent to any developer-controlled server.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Speedcube Trainer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data accessed via Google Drive is used solely to synchronize your own Speedcube Trainer settings and solve history between your devices, is never shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising, creditworthiness, or unrelated purposes.

5. Bluetooth Smart Cubes & Timers (Web Bluetooth)

If you connect a Bluetooth smart cube or timer via your browser's Web Bluetooth API, device communication happens strictly between your browser and your local hardware over Bluetooth Low Energy. Hardware move streams and solve splits are processed locally in your browser (and only included in your optional Google Drive sync file or JSON/CSV exports if saved in your local solve history).

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

You stay in full control of your data at all times:

  • Local browser data: Clear your browser's site data for cubing.cc at any time, or delete individual solves and sessions inside the app.
  • Sign out: Click Sign Out in the Cloud Sync modal to revoke the active browser OAuth session token while keeping your local data on your device.
  • Revoke Google access: Remove Speedcube Trainer's permissions anytime from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete Google Drive app data: In Google Drive, open Settings → Manage apps, locate Speedcube Trainer, and choose Options → Delete hidden app data.

7. Children's Privacy

Speedcube Trainer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone on any developer-operated server. Parents and guardians can inspect all locally stored data directly in browser developer tools or disable optional Google Sign-In simply by not signing in.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how Speedcube Trainer handles local storage or optional Google Drive sync, we will update this page and adjust the "Last updated" date above.
