1st Layer Efficiency & Inspection Guide

🎯 1. Move Count Benchmarks

On a 2x2, corner permutations and orientations have far fewer states than 3x3:

  • 1st Face (Ortega): 99.8% of all scrambles can be solved in ≤ 4 moves (average optimal: 3.1 moves).
  • 1st Layer (CLL/LBL): 95.4% of all scrambles can be solved in ≤ 5 moves (average optimal: 3.8 moves).
  • Rule of thumb: If your planned layer takes 6 or more moves, there is almost certainly a 2- or 3-move solution on another face!

🌈 2. Full Color Neutrality (CN)

Unlike 3x3 where centers are fixed and cross planning across 6 colors takes months, 2x2 has no fixed centers:

  • Every face can be built around whichever corner is already oriented or paired.
  • Being color neutral means you check 6 colors. On average, at least one color already has a pre-formed 2-piece bar!
  • Start by practicing Opposite Neutrality (White + Yellow), then expand to All 6 faces.

⏱️ 3. 15-Second WCA Inspection Routine

In 2x2, world-class cubers plan the entire 1st layer before making their first move:

  1. 0 – 3s (Scan): Rotate the cube quickly. Look for already-made 2-corner bars or matching colors on any side.
  2. 3 – 8s (Select & Trace): Pick the face with the shortest move count. Locate the remaining 2 corners.
  3. 8 – 12s (Rehearse Blind): Visualize every turn in your head. Know where the pieces will land.
  4. 12 – 15s (Grip): Place the cube down, set your fingers on your starting grip, and start the timer ready to execute with zero pauses!

🛠️ 4. Bar Preservation vs. Direct Insertion

Never break an existing 2-piece bar unless it saves moves overall:

  • Direct Insertion: If an isolated corner has its sticker facing the side, use a 3-move trigger like R U R' or F' U' F.
  • Sledgehammer (R' F R F'): When a corner has its target color facing up or needs reorientation, a sledgehammer can insert it while preserving bottom pieces and often orienting top corners!
  • Keyhole Technique: Leave one corner unsolved while you quickly orient the other three, then insert the last corner with a quick trigger.

⚡ 5. Face vs. Layer (The Ortega Transition)

When solving under time pressure:

  • If building a complete layer requires awkward rotations or ≥ 5 moves, build a Face instead (takes 2–3 moves)!
  • Orient the top with OLL (or any CLL alg), then finish with one of the 5 Ortega PBL algorithms.
  • Once comfortable with Ortega, use the 1st Layer Solver in this app to analyze optimal layer solutions and bridge to 1-look CLL.
