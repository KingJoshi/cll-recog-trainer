🎙️ Stackmat G5 Setup & Test Guide BETA

1. Cable & Adapter

  • Plug a 2.5mm TRS to 3.5mm TRS data cable into the Stackmat G5 data port.
  • On MacBook Pro, a USB audio interface with a dedicated 3.5mm Mic / Line-In is recommended, or use a TRS-to-TRRS mic splitter for combo headphone/mic jacks.

2. Connect in App

  1. Enable Stackmat G5 Audio Timer in Settings → Experimental.
  2. Power on your Stackmat G5 in 2-Pad mode.
  3. In Settings → Devices → Timers, pick your input under Audio Input (click ↻ if newly plugged in), then click Connect Stackmat G5 and grant microphone permission.

3. Verify & Test

  1. Check that the signal bar turns green with Signal locked (G4/G5) · State: IDLE.
  2. Switch to Solve mode, rest hands until the Stackmat green LED lights up, lift to start, stop on the pads, and confirm the recorded time matches the Stackmat display to the millisecond (plus pickup / cube / putdown splits if a smart cube is paired).

4. Troubleshooting (macOS & Browser)

  • Open the orange microphone indicator in the macOS menu bar and set Mic Mode → Standard (disable Voice Isolation, which filters out 1200-baud FSK data).
  • In System Settings → Sound → Input, keep input gain around 50–75% and make sure the 2.5mm plug is firmly seated.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Click any shortcut pill and press a key or combo (e.g. K, Shift + R, Alt + 2). Space starts/stops the timer (and stops Virtual Cube solves), and any non-modifier key stops regular solves.

About Cubing.cc

Cubing.cc is an ad-free, fast speedcubing timer, algorithm learner, and scramble analyzer built to help cubers practice cleanly anywhere — online or offline.

  • Ad-Free & Focused: Zero banner ads, zero popups, and a streamlined workflow across Learn, Train, and Solve.
  • Offline-First PWA: Installable on desktop and mobile so timers, scramblers, 2D/3D algorithm guides, and smart cube drills work without an internet connection.
  • Privacy by Default: No analytics scripts, no third-party tracking cookies, and no external server — your solve logs and statistics stay locally on your device.
  • Optional Private Cloud Sync: Google Drive backup is strictly opt-in and stores your sync file solely inside your own Google Drive appDataFolder — nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Open Source (GNU GPL v3.0): View, fork, or contribute on GitHub under GNU GPL v3.0.
